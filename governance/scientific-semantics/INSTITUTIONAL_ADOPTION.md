# Institutional adoption policy

BioNexus does not count downloads, mentions, forks, trial registrations, or
maintainer self-use as institutional adoption.

An organization appears in the public adoption registry only after submitting
a versioned declaration that identifies:

- the exact BNS-019 release digest;
- adoption role: `experimental_implementer`, `production_producer`,
  `production_consumer`, or `standards_liaison`;
- named implementation and immutable source or deployment evidence;
- conformance result artifact and SHA-256 when applicable;
- scope, exclusions, and known semantic loss;
- accountable organizational contact; and
- permission to publish the declaration.

`experimental_implementer` means an integration trial, not organizational
endorsement. Production roles require a reproducible round trip or consumer
acceptance artifact. A standards liaison indicates coordination only and must
not be marketed as adoption or endorsement.

Declarations expire after 18 months unless renewed against a supported release.
Withdrawal, failed renewal, digest mismatch, or material misrepresentation
changes the entry to `withdrawn`, `expired`, or `revoked`; history remains
public. Commercial sponsorship does not create or upgrade an adoption record.
