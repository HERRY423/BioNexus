# Independent assurance and certification policy

Certification is suspended until the assurance registry contains at least one
recognized, independent conformity assessment body (CAB) and the governance
validator confirms all readiness gates.

The SSC owns technical requirements and public conformance fixtures. A separate
assurance scheme oversight function owns assessor eligibility, surveillance,
certificate registry format, complaints, and assurance-process appeals. It does
not alter technical meaning. Recognized CABs execute assessment against an exact
frozen release and independently issue, suspend, expire, or revoke certificates.

Minimum CAB evidence includes:

- legal identity and accountable officers;
- competence for software conformance and the relevant scientific domain;
- public impartiality and conflict policy;
- separation from BioNexus commercial control and the applicant;
- reproducible assessment procedure and assessor identity;
- complaints and appeal process independent of the original assessor;
- certificate transparency and revocation publication; and
- professional liability and records-retention policy appropriate to scope.

Recognition is not accreditation and BioNexus does not claim ISO accreditation.
If future accreditation or peer recognition is claimed, the exact accreditor,
scope, identifier, status, and evidence must be published and independently
verified.

Until these conditions hold, BCTK remains a development diagnostic,
`badge_eligible=false`, and all certification decisions remain `NOT_ASSESSED`.
