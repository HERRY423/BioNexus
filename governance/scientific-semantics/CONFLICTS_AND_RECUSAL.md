# Conflicts, funding, and recusal

Every Council member, independent observer, assurance overseer, and CAB assessor
publishes a disclosure at appointment and renews it at least annually and after
any material change.

Disclosures include current employer and related companies, board or executive
roles, material equity, relevant paid consulting, grants or contracts, product
maintainership, certification applicants assessed, and immediate-family
interests known to the participant.

Recusal is mandatory when a decision directly affects:

- the participant's employer, client, product, or certification application;
- an implementation the participant substantially authored;
- a party from which the participant received material compensation during the
  previous 12 months; or
- an unresolved personal or organizational dispute that a reasonable observer
  would consider material.

A recused person may provide factual information when requested but does not
chair, deliberate privately, vote, select reviewers, or count toward quorum.
Recusal and its scope are recorded. Failure to disclose may invalidate the
affected decision and trigger removal review.

Aggregated funding sources, restricted grants, in-kind infrastructure, and
sponsor benefits are reported quarterly. Funding never buys a Council seat,
technical vote, shortened review, certification outcome, or endorsement.
