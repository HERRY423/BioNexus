# Change governance

Every proposal MUST identify a producer and consumer use case, define the
scientific meaning without method-specific thresholds, classify compatibility,
and add or update language-neutral conformance cases.

The following require explicit scientific and interoperability review:

- collision with an existing name or external convention;
- ambiguity in biological unit, matrix state, claim strength, evidence class,
  confound, or warrant meaning;
- a new alias that could conceal information loss;
- deprecation or removal; and
- a move to `stable`.

Review status, authorship, or repository ownership is not an endorsement. A
future neutral governance body may assume stewardship without renaming the
public fields or changing their released meanings.
