# Scientific Semantics neutral governance

BioNexus now has a concrete transition path from project-owned vocabulary to a
neutral scientific contract:

```text
Scientific Semantics Council     Commercial operators       Assurance / CABs
technical meaning                funding and products       independent assessment
registry and schemas             pricing and marketing      certificate + revocation
RFC and technical appeals        trademark policy           certification appeals
            \                         |                         /
             +------ explicit constitutional firewalls ------+
```

The critical current fact is that the Council is not yet independently formed.
The machine state therefore remains:

- `FORMING`;
- `NOT_YET_ESTABLISHED` independence;
- zero verified institutional adopters;
- zero recognized conformity assessment bodies; and
- certification and badging suspended.

The complete charter, role matrix, conflict policy, institutional adoption
contract, assurance policy, schemas, and registries are under
`governance/scientific-semantics/`. BNS-022 makes their failure states normative
for the BioNexus project. `scripts/validate_semantic_governance.py` rejects
claim inflation and cross-branch authority overlap.

This follows established institutional patterns without claiming affiliation:
open technical decisions and formal objections; technical authority separated
from business administration; and conformity-assessment policy separated from
the bodies that perform assessment.
